Adapted for Niks Piano. Uses only velocity layer 8, sampled in minor thirds.
The recordings are retuned with the upstream Retuned offsets, trimmed,
peak-normalized, faded, and resampled to stereo 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM.
The original 17 training samples (MIDI 36–84) are also used in Free Piano.
Free Piano adds 13 recorded anchors for MIDI 21–33 and 87–108. The new
bass samples retain up to seven seconds and the new treble samples up to
four seconds of the recorded decay. Free Piano playback applies fine tuning
based on the measured fundamental of each recording.
